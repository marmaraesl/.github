# Embedded Systems & Linux Club (ESL)

We are a student-driven engineering community at Marmara University focused on low-level software, hardware, and open-source technologies.

### What We Do
* **Bare-Metal & RTOS:** Writing C/C++ directly on hardware (STM32, ESP32, RP2040).
* **Embedded Linux & Systems:** Building custom Linux distributions (Buildroot/Yocto), kernel modules, device drivers, and userspace tools.
* **Hardware & PCB Design:** Designing custom boards, reading schematics, and debugging protocols (UART, SPI, I2C, CAN) with logic analyzers.
* **Build & Collaborate:** A workspace where students find teammates, form their own project squads, and ship real hardware/software projects together.

### Get Involved
Whether you are trying to blink your first LED or compiling a custom kernel, you are welcome to join our table, pitch your project idea, and build with us.
